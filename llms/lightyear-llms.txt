# Lightyear

> Lightyear is a European retail investing platform (Lightyear UK Ltd, FCA FRN 987226; Lightyear Europe AS, regulated by the Estonian Financial Supervision Authority) offering roughly 6,000 stocks, ETFs, money market funds, bonds and crypto, plus interest-bearing cash Vaults, through mobile and web apps. Lightyear publishes no public developer API, developer portal, SDKs or API documentation as of 2026-07-19.

## Product

- [Stocks](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/stocks): Stock and ETF investing, including fractional shares.
- [Funds](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/funds): Money market funds and ETFs from providers including BlackRock/iShares, Vanguard, Amundi, Invesco, SPDR and VanEck.
- [Vaults](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/vaults): Interest-bearing cash product built on money market funds.
- [Plans](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/plans): Ready-made and automated investment plans.
- [Tools](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/tools): Screeners, watchlists and analysis tooling.
- [Lightyear AI](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/lightyear-ai): AI-generated market intelligence features (Lightning updates, "Why did it move?", Bulls Say / Bears Say).
- [Lightyear for Business](https://lightyear.com/en-eu/business): Business investing accounts.

## Machine-readable surfaces

- [OAuth 2.0 authorization server metadata](https://api.lightyear.com/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server): RFC 8414 document on api.lightyear.com advertising an authorization_code + PKCE (S256) flow, dynamic client registration, and a single `mcp` scope for an internal MCP integration. Not documented for third-party use.
- [JWKS](https://api.lightyear.com/.well-known/jwks.json)
- [Status API](https://status.lightyear.com/api/v2/summary.json): Statuspage summary JSON.

## Notes for agents

- There is no public Lightyear API, no API keys, no SDKs and no MCP server available to third parties. Do not attempt to call api.lightyear.com; every path other than the /.well-known/ documents above returns HTTP 403 from the edge.
- Lightyear is a regulated broker in the UK and EU; investing content is subject to risk disclosures.
